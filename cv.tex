\section{Industrial Research and Product Development -- Utopi Ltd}
The following descriptions are intentionally high level for a public CV. Client data, commercial thresholds, proprietary calibration choices and implementation details are omitted.

\subsection{Energy Benchmarking and Technical Efficiency}
Developed weather-normalised energy benchmarking methods that combine change-point modelling, stochastic frontier analysis and efficiency estimation. Built quality-control logic and reporting outputs to support portfolio-level comparison, identification of avoidable consumption and operational decision-making.

\subsection{Mould-Risk Analytics}
Implemented an hourly mould-risk model based on published Ojanen/VTT methodology, using indoor environmental telemetry to estimate conditions associated with surface mould development and to support early operational intervention.

\subsection{Legionella Proxy Risk}
Developed a risk-prioritisation model that combines occupancy/stagnation evidence with thermal behaviour to identify locations that warrant attention. The model supports targeted operational checks without exposing site-specific thresholds or internal control rules.

\subsection{Heat-Loss Estimation}
Developed inverse cooling-event models that estimate relative heat-loss behaviour from passive temperature decay. The method extracts suitable cooling periods from telemetry, fits space-level thermal-decay models and applies model-quality checks before interpretation.

\subsection{Anomalous Heating and Rogue-Heater Detection}
Developed statistical models for identifying indoor-temperature behaviour inconsistent with expected outdoor conditions, prior indoor state and occupancy evidence. The work supports investigation of portable or unmanaged heating and other atypical thermal behaviour.

\subsection{Unoccupied-Heating Analytics}
Developed portfolio analytics to identify heating that persists when occupancy evidence is low, enabling operational teams to distinguish likely occupancy-driven demand from potentially avoidable heating.

\subsection{Utopi Index / Building Energy Performance Score}
Developed and productionised a multi-signal building-performance scoring framework that converts telemetry-derived performance indicators into a concise operational index. Implemented the analytical logic as a deployable cloud service with validation and model-QA workflows.
